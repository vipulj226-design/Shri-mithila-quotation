Service Explanations in Simple Terms

Domain vs. Hosting

Domain: Your online identity address (e.g. shrimithila.in).
Hosting: Your 24/7 digital warehouse where files, pictures and website data live.
Simple example: Domain = Your business address | Hosting = Your shop building.

Custom Code vs. Templates

Instead of rigid ready-made themes, a 100% custom-coded website provides instant load times, clean code, no unwanted monthly plugin fees, and maximum control over design.

Why Maintenance is Mandatory

Web technology evolves continuously. Maintenance protects your website from security bugs, keeps servers updated, and ensures you have a dedicated developer for product changes.

Basic SEO vs. Advanced SEO

Basic SEO builds the road and signs so search engines can find and index your site. Advanced SEO is active marketing to compete for competitive top-page keywords over time.
